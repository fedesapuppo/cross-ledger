Why we built this

We started Cross Ledger because we know what Latin American professionals are capable of. The talent is here: same skill, same rigor, same English. What's been missing is a way to get those professionals in front of US small businesses without the friction of time zones, bad sourcing, or expensive intermediaries. That's the company.
