The Process

How it works

Five steps from discovery call to your new bookkeeper. We do the hard part.

1

Tell us what you need

A 30-minute call where we go through your tools, your hours, your books, and what's gone wrong before. We use this to build the candidate scorecard.

2

We source and screen

We reach out to LATAM bookkeepers who match your scorecard. Every candidate goes through structured interviews, an English-fluency check, and a practical simulation in QuickBooks.

3

You see candidates on video before you commit

Every candidate comes with a short intro video. They introduce themselves, walk through their experience, and explain a basic accounting concept on camera. Rule someone out in two minutes instead of two interviews.

4

You interview the ones you like

We schedule, you interview, you decide. We're in the loop only as much as you want us to be.

5

You hire them directly. We charge a one-time placement fee.

No monthly markup, no payroll relationship, no ongoing fee. You own the hire.
