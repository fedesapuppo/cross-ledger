Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you book a discovery call or contact us, we collect information you provide directly: your name, email address, business name, and details about your bookkeeping needs. We do not collect information automatically through cookies or tracking technologies beyond basic analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Schedule and conduct discovery calls
  • Match you with suitable bookkeeper candidates
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Improve our placement process

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant details about your business needs with bookkeeper candidates during the placement process, only with your consent.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at fedesapuppo@hotmail.com.

6. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at fedesapuppo@hotmail.com.
